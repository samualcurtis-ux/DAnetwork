Executive Summary

23,123 broadband serviceable locations in Osage County. 14,909 (64%) lack any wireline broadband provider. 137 fiber routes and 307 infrastructure points are under construction or built (source: OIS/Eric Short, April 2026). 11 AtLink tower sites provide wireless coverage. 16 ARPA 3.0/BEAD planned tower sites identified. $148.7M in active or pending grant funding.
